Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 17, 2026

Who I am

This website, faizanahmedseo.com, is operated by Faizan Ahmed, an independent SEO consultant. In this policy, “I”, “me” and “my” mean Faizan Ahmed.

Information I collect

  • Information you provide. If you use the free SEO audit, I process the website URL you enter. If you request a full report or contact me, I may also receive your name and email address.
  • Booking details. If you book a call, scheduling is handled by Calendly. They collect the details you enter there under their own privacy policy.
  • Usage data. Like most sites, basic technical data (such as pages viewed, browser type, device type and approximate location) may be recorded by hosting and analytics tools to keep the site working and understand what is useful.
  • Your theme choice. If you switch between light and dark mode, your preference is stored in your browser’s local storage so the site remembers it. It is not sent to me.

How I use information

  • To run the SEO audit and show you the results.
  • To reply to your messages and send any report you asked for.
  • To keep the site secure, fast and working properly.
  • To understand, in aggregate, which pages are useful so I can improve them.

I do not sell your personal information, and I do not use it for automated decisions that affect you.

Third-party services

This site relies on a few outside services, each with its own privacy practices:

  • Vercel hosts the site and may log request data such as IP address for security and performance.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces used on the site, which means your browser requests font files from Google.
  • Calendly handles call booking when you follow the “Book a call” link.
  • Social platforms (LinkedIn, Instagram, X, Facebook) are linked from this site. They apply their own policies once you click through.

Cookies and local storage

This site does not set advertising cookies. It uses browser local storage for your theme preference. Third-party services listed above may set their own cookies when you interact with them. You can block or delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings.

How long I keep information

I keep emails and messages only as long as needed to respond and follow up on your request. Audit URLs are used to generate your results. You can ask me to delete anything you have sent me at any time.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, or to object to certain uses of it. To make a request, contact me using the details below and I will respond within a reasonable time.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but I take reasonable steps to protect the information I handle.

Children

This site is intended for businesses and adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy? Reach me on LinkedIn or book a short call.
