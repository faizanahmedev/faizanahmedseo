01 / The start

A blank domain.
A full-stack SEO education.

Back in 2023, I started Ecashinsight from scratch — a niche blog focused on digital payments, Cash App, and Venmo.

No inherited audience. No existing rankings. I owned the whole process: finding the right keywords, building topical maps, optimizing content, and working through on-page, technical, and off-page SEO.

The goal was simple: build it, grow it, and understand how every part connects.

03 / The real lesson
Not every growth story is a straight line

Then the algorithm changed.
The learning didn’t stop.

Algorithm updates knocked the site down. I worked through what went wrong, then made the decision to sell it.

The outcome wasn’t just a set of rankings. It was a first-hand understanding of the whole SEO ecosystem — how research, content, technical foundations, and external signals work together, and what happens when the landscape shifts.

“Doing it end to end taught me how the whole SEO ecosystem works.”
