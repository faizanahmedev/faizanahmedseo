01 / The starting point

Essentially invisible.
Not by the product’s fault.

When I joined The American Jackets in February 2025, the site was essentially invisible in search — fewer than 500 monthly organic visits, minimal indexed pages, and almost no keyword rankings to speak of.

The product side was never the problem. It was built on a solid concept: premium leather and varsity jackets for the US market. What it had been left with was compounding technical debt.

That debt wasn’t a background issue. It was actively suppressing the site’s organic reach.

02 / The work

Audit first.
Then the blockers.

Over the following months, the fix ran in order: find what was broken, clear the things stopping the site from being crawled and indexed, then give the content a structure worth ranking.

01

Complete technical SEO audit

A full read of the site before anything changed — what search engines could crawl, what they could index, and what was being held back.

02

Crawl and indexation blockers resolved

The most critical ones first: the issues that were stopping pages from being found and included in the index at all.

03

On-page content structure rebuilt

Product, category, and informational pages given a structure that both shoppers and search engines could follow.

04

Schema markup implemented

Pages described in a form search engines could read directly, so the store’s own content could speak for itself.

03 / The result

Nearly 5,000 keywords.
Across every page type.

By 2026 the site is pulling 3,500–4,000 monthly organic visits and ranking for nearly 5,000 keywords — spread across its product, category, and informational pages, not concentrated in a single corner of the store.

04 / The takeaway
Technical debt is a traffic problem

The product was already there.
Search just couldn’t see it.

Nothing about the jackets changed. What changed was whether a crawler could reach the pages, understand them, and trust them enough to include them.

A complete audit, the critical crawl and indexation blockers cleared, the on-page structure rebuilt, and schema markup in place — that is the whole difference between <500 visits a month and nearly 5,000 ranking keywords.
